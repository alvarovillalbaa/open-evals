# Context Packs

Context is separated into:

- Execution context: what the target actually knew.
- Normative context: what should govern correctness.
- Outcome context: what happened later.

Outcome context must not leak into judges that evaluate what the target could reasonably have known at response time.

Authority order:

```text
legal and safety
> mandatory platform policy
> mandatory company policy
> company preferences
> user preferences
> conversation style
```
